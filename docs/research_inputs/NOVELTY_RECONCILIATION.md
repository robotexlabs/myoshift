# Neutral novelty reconciliation

Retrospective public copy of the MyoShift decision context. Internal attribution, unrelated portfolio judgements and unsupported novelty claims are omitted; the exact original remains excluded privately.

The inherited reviews disagreed on whether to build immediately or first clear a differentiation gate. The shared concern was that generic cross-person decoding already has substantial prior art. The current release does not resolve that broader novelty gate: decoder superiority is withdrawn and threshold-swept or matched-recall comparisons remain untested.

[EMGBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23625) and the [generic neuromotor interface paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09255-w) are the retained comparison context. The [public research context](RESEARCH_CONTEXT.md) records the pilot data reference.
