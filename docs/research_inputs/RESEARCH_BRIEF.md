# Neutral research brief

Retrospective public copy of the MyoShift portion of the inherited portfolio brief. Unrelated project material and internal attribution are omitted; the exact original remains excluded privately.

Question: can inexpensive calibration and abstention preserve gesture-command recall under participant, session and electrode shift while reducing false commands? Required gates are usable public data, explicit file licences, close prior art, independent ground truth and a measured comparison against credible reference decoders.

The broad novelty proposal was unverified. The repaired pilot is a fixed, descriptive negative result, with no clinical or deployment claim. See [public research context](RESEARCH_CONTEXT.md) for resolved primary citations and current scope.
