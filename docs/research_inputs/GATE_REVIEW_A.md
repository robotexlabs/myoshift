Retrospective public summary of the MyoShift section of one inherited gate review. Internal attribution and unresolved citation tokens are omitted; the exact original remains excluded privately. Recommendations are historical proposals, not completed validation.

The review recommended a differentiation gate against generic neuromotor decoding and an explicit abstention evaluation. The [generic neuromotor interface paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09255-w) is the resolved primary comparison. The current negative pilot does not establish the proposed advantage.

Dataset eligibility and licence scope must be checked at the file level. Only the [GRABMyoFlow extension](https://doi.org/10.13026/ah52-zq51) pilot was used here. Broader datasets and clinical validation remain outside the measured result.
